## darts-o-mat v0.0.4

### New game mode: Cricket
- **Cricket** is the third game mode next to X01 and Around the Clock: numbers 15 to 20 and the bull. A single counts one mark, a double two, a triple three; the outer bull one, the bullseye two. Three marks close a number; further marks score while an opponent hasn't closed it yet.
- **Two variants:** in **Standard** the points go to the thrower and the most points win; in **Cut-Throat** the points go to every opponent who hasn't closed the number and the fewest points win. A tie wins in both.
- Legs, sets, bull-off, takeout and the time per turn work as in the other modes.
- **Game page:** each player card shows the marks per number (/, X, ⓧ). The numbers that are still open are highlighted on the board; numbers everyone has closed are dimmed.
- **Statistics:** marks per round (MPR) and the best leg in darts, with a Cricket filter on the statistics page. Tournament standings show the MPR.
- **Open games:** a filter by variant; the form shown for a player is their MPR. A quick match plays Standard.

### Playing
- **Rematch:** a finished game has a "Rematch" button that creates a new game with the same settings. Your players sit on their boards, the other players with an account are invited. Whoever clicks it second joins the same rematch instead of creating another one.
- **Leaving during the bull-off:** a player can now leave while bulling off and is out of the bull-off, so nobody holds up the others.
- **Accent colors:** teal, cyan, indigo and fuchsia were too close to other colors and have been removed. If you had one of them, you get the default color again until you choose a new one in your settings.
- Game pages with many darts load faster.

### Camera (darts-o-cam)
- **More accurate at the wires:** the tip is moved back 0.5 mm instead of 1 mm by default. On a test series of 32 darts placed right at the wires of the triple and double rings, all 32 now score the right field.
- **A hint when libjpeg-turbo is missing:** darts-o-cam tells you when it runs without it and therefore needs much more computing power.

### Running a server
- **Statistics on the admin page:** accounts (active, new, disabled), guests, games (running, completed, aborted, per mode), tournaments, darts (of the last 30 days, with the share from cameras) and boards.
- **Much faster with many games:** new database indexes. With 7.5 million darts in PostgreSQL, loading a game's darts takes 0.4 ms instead of 262 ms, the list of open games 2.7 ms instead of 2.8 s.
- **SQLite is much faster:** the database now uses its write-ahead log; saving a dart takes about 0.2 ms instead of 90 ms. Next to `darts-o-mat.db` you will now see `darts-o-mat.db-wal` and `darts-o-mat.db-shm`: they belong to the database, keep them together (stop the server before copying the files for a backup).
- **PostgreSQL connections are limited** to 25 per replica and kept open, so several replicas no longer use up PostgreSQL's connections under load.
- **Migrations survive a stop:** if the server was stopped while it migrated the database (for example a restart by systemd or a rolling update), every later start could fail with "index already exists". A migration that has started now always runs to its end.
- Dependencies updated.

### Updating
- The database is migrated automatically when the server starts (Cricket, rematch, new indexes). On a large PostgreSQL database creating the indexes can take a moment.
- **API:** `GET /api/games` no longer contains `rematch_id`; `GET /api/games/{id}` still does.
- **Building from source:** the build tag `noturbojpeg` is gone; darts-o-cam always looks for libjpeg-turbo at runtime and falls back to Go's decoder without it.
